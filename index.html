    How it works · about a minute

    Four flashes, one strip

    No app, no sign-up. Pick a booth, let the camera in, and walk out with a strip of you in your favourite era.

    1. 01
      tap to step inside

      01Pick your era

      Scroll the library and tap a booth. Every era has its own colours, stickers and strip.

    2. 02
      LIVE ✓ Camera on

      02Let the camera in

      Allow camera access and see yourself live. Nothing is uploaded; your photos stay on your device.

    3. 03 321
      click!

      03Strike four poses

      Pick your cut: a classic 4-shot strip, a 3-shot trio, a 2×2 grid or a six-pack. Then three, two, one, flash.

    4. 04
      ↓ Save strip

      04Save your strip

      Watch it feed out of the printer in the era's frame, then save it as an image or go again as many times as you like.
